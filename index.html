What is Jio-bp?+

Jio-bp is a mobility and energy partnership between Reliance Industries Limited and bp, serving customers through fuel retail, EV charging, CNG, fleet solutions and more.

How can I find a Jio-bp mobility station near me?+

Use our station locator to discover nearby Jio-bp mobility stations and the services available at each location.

Does Jio-bp offer doorstep diesel delivery?+

Fuel4U provides doorstep diesel delivery for enterprise customers, with a focus on reliable and compliant service.

Where can I charge my electric vehicle?+

Jio-bp pulse is building a network of EV charging stations. Check the pulse app for charger locations and availability.

How do I join the Jio-bp network as a partner?+

Visit Partner with Us to learn about opportunities for fleet owners and mobility station partners.

What is the RewardMeter Program?+

RewardMeter lets customers earn rewards when they fuel up at participating Jio-bp stations. Check the program for current earning and redemption details.
